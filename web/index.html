Faster, more consistent home loans — with an officer in control

A home loan today touches a dozen documents, three kinds of data and five or six people. Sanctum shows how a lakehouse and one well-governed AI agent can compress that into hours for clean cases and put officer attention where it matters.

Act 1 — Bring the data together

Credit officers work from three worlds that never meet in one place. The lakehouse joins them so every decision starts from the same complete picture.

Documents

RBI circulars, the bank's credit policy, and applicant KYC, salary slips, bank statements, ITRs, property papers. Read once, turned into searchable facts.

Bank records

Customer profile, accounts, existing loans, repayment history from core banking. Refreshed daily, held in one governed store.

External checks

Credit bureau, CERSAI charge search, PAN and Aadhaar verification, GST, account aggregator. Pulled on demand, logged with cost and time.

Act 2 — Put an agent on top

The agent does the reading, checking and arithmetic that eat officer hours. It cites every fact and every rule, then hands over a file ready to decide.

1. IntakeIs the file complete? Do documents match what was declared?
2. GatherPull bank records; call bureau, CERSAI, KYC
3. PolicyApply current RBI norms and bank policy
4. RecommendApprove, refer or decline — with reasons
5. OfficerHuman reviews, edits conditions, signs off
6. DisburseLegal, valuation, mortgage, tranche release

Faster

Clean files: recommendation in minutes, not days of queueing between desks. Targets confirmed in a pilot with your data.

More consistent

Same rules, same evidence, every branch. Policy changes reach every decision on day one.

Auditable

Each recommendation lists the data and policy clause behind it. Overrides are recorded with reasons.

Where people stay in charge

The officer approves every sanction. The agent escalates on weak documents, policy breaches, existing charges on the property, or low confidence. Declines are never automatic.

About & FAQ

Sanctum is a point-of-view demo of agentic home loan approval for retail lending teams at Indian banks. It uses synthetic data only and does not connect to any bureau or government system.

Does the AI approve loans?

No. It prepares the file and recommends. A credit officer approves, changes or declines every case.

Which rules does it apply?

In the demo: RBI loan-to-value limits for housing loans (90% up to ₹30 lakh, 80% to ₹75 lakh, 75% above), plus illustrative bank limits for FOIR and bureau score. Your credit policy replaces these.

What happens when documents are poor or inconsistent?

The agent flags the mismatch, lists what it needs, and routes the file to an officer rather than guessing.

How do you keep it consistent when policy changes?

Policy documents live in the knowledge layer. Update the document and every later decision cites the new version.

Where does data sit?

In the bank's own cloud project in India, with access control, masking and a full audit trail.

What would a pilot look like?

One product, a few branches, shadow mode first: agent recommends, officers decide as usual, and we compare outcomes and turnaround.
